# Hogar Dorado

> **Una nostalgia que parece un sueño.**

Hogar Dorado is an experimental Minecraft Bedrock resource pack exploring nostalgia, dreamcore atmosphere, warm golden sunsets, indigo nights, and subtle environmental depth while preserving Minecraft's recognizable visual identity.

## Purpose

The project has two connected but distinct lines of exploration:

- **Artistic:** create a warm, familiar, quietly mysterious atmosphere that accompanies players' own memories and new experiences.
- **Learning and economic experiment:** learn about digital product development, AI-assisted creation, community discovery, distribution, and whether the project can generate supplemental income. No income or audience is assumed.

## Artistic roots

- Golden sunsets and warm afternoon light.
- Indigo nights and subtle mystery.
- Atmosphere over spectacle; visual harmony and comfort during longer play sessions.
- Preserve the recognizable identity of Minecraft.
- The pack should accompany players' experiences rather than demand constant attention.
- Nostalgia should not recreate one person's exact past; it should leave room for each player to find their own memories and make new ones.

## Current status

**Early beta / experimental.** The pack is not considered ready for public release. Features, compatibility, visual balance, and presentation still require iteration and in-game testing.

The current generated cover concept is an **art-direction reference**, not evidence of how the pack currently looks in-game. Public preview screenshots must come from the actual pack running in Minecraft.
